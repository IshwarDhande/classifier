# Stage 1 — Semantic Scenario Inventory: I

**Response Category:** `I` — Indifferent  
**Semantic Subtypes:** 4  
**Scenario Count:** 20

---

## 1. Purpose

This inventory defines distinct behavioral situations in which a user's response belongs to the `I` Response Category.

`I` represents a response in which there is insufficient evidence of clear behavioral acceptance or meaningful resistance toward the intervention.

The scenarios below describe semantic situations, not linguistic realizations.

---

# 2. I-01 — Neutral Acknowledgement

## I-01-S01 — Simple Acknowledgement

**Scenario:** The user acknowledges the intervention without indicating whether they will accept or resist it.

**Behavioral core:** Acknowledgement without behavioral direction.

**Boundary:** Acknowledgement alone does not establish acceptance.

## I-01-S02 — Recognition Without Commitment

**Scenario:** The user recognizes or confirms awareness of the recommendation but does not indicate willingness to follow it.

**Behavioral core:** Recognition + no clear commitment.

**Boundary:** Awareness is not equivalent to acceptance.

## I-01-S03 — Receipt of Recommendation

**Scenario:** The user indicates that they have received or understood the intervention without expressing a behavioral position toward it.

**Behavioral core:** Receipt/understanding + unresolved response.

**Boundary:** Understanding does not establish cooperation.

## I-01-S04 — Neutral Response to Repeated Intervention

**Scenario:** After a repeated intervention, the user acknowledges it without showing clear acceptance or resistance.

**Behavioral core:** Acknowledgement + unresolved behavioral response.

**Boundary:** Repetition alone does not make the response negative.

## I-01-S05 — Neutral Acknowledgement of Suggested Action

**Scenario:** The user acknowledges a suggested action but provides no evidence that they intend to perform or reject it.

**Behavioral core:** Acknowledgement + no behavioral commitment.

**Boundary:** The response remains Indifferent because willingness is unresolved.

---

# 3. I-02 — Uncertain Position

## I-02-S01 — Uncertain About Whether to Act

**Scenario:** The user expresses genuine uncertainty about whether they will follow the recommended action.

**Behavioral core:** Uncertainty + unresolved willingness.

**Boundary:** No meaningful resistance is established.

## I-02-S02 — Considering the Recommendation

**Scenario:** The user indicates that they are still considering the intervention and have not formed a clear behavioral position.

**Behavioral core:** Deliberation + unresolved acceptance.

**Boundary:** Consideration without resistance remains Indifferent.

## I-02-S03 — Unsure About Future Cooperation

**Scenario:** The user is unsure whether they will cooperate with the recommendation at a later point.

**Behavioral core:** Uncertain future cooperation.

**Boundary:** The response lacks sufficient evidence for either Positive or Negative.

## I-02-S04 — Ambiguous Position After Explanation

**Scenario:** After receiving an explanation, the user remains uncertain about whether to accept the recommendation.

**Behavioral core:** Continued uncertainty + unresolved behavioral response.

**Boundary:** Uncertainty is not treated as disagreement unless resistance is expressed.

## I-02-S05 — Deferring a Behavioral Decision Without Resistance

**Scenario:** The user postpones deciding whether to follow the recommendation without expressing opposition.

**Behavioral core:** Deferred decision + unresolved willingness.

**Boundary:** Deferral alone does not constitute Negative resistance.

---

# 4. I-03 — Non-Commitment

## I-03-S01 — No Commitment to Follow Through

**Scenario:** The user responds to the recommendation but does not indicate whether they intend to carry it out.

**Behavioral core:** No clear commitment + no clear resistance.

**Boundary:** The absence of commitment does not by itself establish rejection.

## I-03-S02 — Open-Ended Future Response

**Scenario:** The user leaves future action open without making a commitment or expressing resistance.

**Behavioral core:** Open future position + unresolved willingness.

**Boundary:** The response remains below the acceptance threshold required for Positive.

## I-03-S03 — Non-Committal Response to Direct Recommendation

**Scenario:** The user responds to a direct recommendation without accepting, rejecting, or committing to it.

**Behavioral core:** Non-commitment + neutral behavioral direction.

**Boundary:** The lack of a decision is not itself resistance.

## I-03-S04 — Acknowledgement Without Follow-Through Intention

**Scenario:** The user acknowledges the recommendation but gives no indication of intending to follow through.

**Behavioral core:** Acknowledgement + absent commitment.

**Boundary:** Acknowledgement remains insufficient for Positive classification.

## I-03-S05 — Unresolved Willingness After Discussion

**Scenario:** After discussing the intervention, the user remains unwilling to make a clear commitment while also not resisting it.

**Behavioral core:** Unresolved willingness + no dominant resistance.

**Boundary:** The absence of commitment is the defining feature; explicit reluctance would move the response toward Negative.

---

# 5. I-04 — Insufficient Behavioral Evidence

## I-04-S01 — Minimal Behavioral Signal

**Scenario:** The user's response provides too little behavioral information to determine whether they accept or resist the intervention.

**Behavioral core:** Insufficient evidence + unresolved response.

**Boundary:** The classification is based on lack of behavioral evidence, not on response length alone.

## I-04-S02 — Ambiguous Short Response in Context

**Scenario:** A short response could plausibly indicate acceptance, neutrality, or resistance, and the available context does not resolve the behavioral meaning.

**Behavioral core:** Ambiguous behavioral evidence.

**Boundary:** Do not infer a stronger category without sufficient evidence.

## I-04-S03 — Response Not Directed Clearly Toward Intervention

**Scenario:** The user responds conversationally, but it is unclear whether the response expresses a behavioral reaction toward the intervention.

**Behavioral core:** Unclear target of behavioral response.

**Boundary:** Lack of clear direction toward the intervention prevents stronger classification.

## I-04-S04 — Incomplete Behavioral Position

**Scenario:** The user provides part of a response but does not reveal enough of their behavioral position to establish acceptance or resistance.

**Behavioral core:** Incomplete evidence.

**Boundary:** Missing evidence should not be replaced by speculation.

## I-04-S05 — Context Does Not Resolve Ambiguity

**Scenario:** Relevant conversational history is available, but even after considering it, the user's behavioral response remains insufficiently determined.

**Behavioral core:** Persistent ambiguity after contextual interpretation.

**Boundary:** The response remains Indifferent when neither acceptance nor resistance can be established.

# 6. Cross-Scenario Coverage

| Subtype | Primary semantic focus |
|---|---|
| `I-01` | Neutral acknowledgement without behavioral direction |
| `I-02` | Genuine uncertainty about behavioral position |
| `I-03` | Absence of behavioral commitment without resistance |
| `I-04` | Insufficient evidence to determine acceptance or resistance |

The inventory covers:

- neutral acknowledgement;
- awareness without acceptance;
- uncertainty;
- deliberation;
- deferred decisions;
- non-commitment;
- unresolved willingness;
- ambiguous behavioral evidence;
- incomplete behavioral positions;
- contextually unresolved responses.

Linguistic realization diversity is introduced separately during Stage 3A.

---

# 7. I Boundary Coverage

The central distinction is:

```text
I
No sufficient evidence of acceptance
and no meaningful behavioral resistance
        ↓
P
Behavioral acceptance / cooperation is evident

I
No meaningful behavioral resistance
        ↓
N
Behaviorally meaningful resistance is evident
```

The following distinctions are especially important:

```text
Delayed action ≠ automatically I
Uncertainty ≠ automatically N
Non-commitment ≠ automatically N
Acknowledgement ≠ automatically P
Short response ≠ automatically I
```

The behavioral evidence available in context must determine the category.

---

# 8. Scenario-Level Semantic Requirements

Each scenario represents one dominant behavioral situation.

The following semantic regions remain distinct:

```text
Neutral Acknowledgement
        ≠
Uncertain Position
        ≠
Non-Commitment
        ≠
Insufficient Behavioral Evidence
```

`I-04` should not become a generic fallback for difficult examples. It represents cases where the available evidence genuinely does not establish a stronger behavioral interpretation.

Temporal delay, politeness, shortness, and conversational style are realization or scenario characteristics rather than independent Response Category definitions.
