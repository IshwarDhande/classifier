# Stage 1 — Semantic Scenario Inventory: VN

**Response Category:** `VN` — Very Negative  
**Semantic Subtypes:** 4  
**Scenario Count:** 20

---

## 1. Purpose

This inventory defines distinct behavioral situations in which a user's response belongs to the `VN` Response Category.

`VN` represents strong rejection, explicit opposition, hostile reactance, or active disengagement from the intervention or interaction. The defining characteristic is a level of negative behavioral response that exceeds ordinary reluctance, mild refusal, disagreement, or recoverable resistance.

The scenarios below describe semantic situations, not linguistic realizations.

---

# 2. VN-01 — Strong Rejection

## VN-01-S01 — Explicit Rejection of the Recommendation

**Scenario:** The user clearly rejects the recommended action and communicates that they will not cooperate.

**Behavioral core:** Strong refusal + explicit non-cooperation.

**Boundary:** Rejection is stronger than ordinary or mild refusal.

## VN-01-S02 — Firm Rejection of the Intervention

**Scenario:** The user firmly rejects the intervention itself rather than merely expressing hesitation about one aspect of it.

**Behavioral core:** Strong intervention rejection.

**Boundary:** The response demonstrates clear opposition beyond recoverable resistance.

## VN-01-S03 — Repeated Rejection After Continued Intervention

**Scenario:** The user strongly rejects the recommendation after the agent continues presenting the intervention.

**Behavioral core:** Persistent rejection + reduced willingness to cooperate.

**Boundary:** The repeated rejection demonstrates stronger resistance than a single mild refusal.

## VN-01-S04 — Rejection of Further Recommendation

**Scenario:** The user explicitly rejects receiving further recommendations concerning the current behavior.

**Behavioral core:** Strong rejection of continued intervention.

**Boundary:** The rejection extends beyond the immediate recommendation.

## VN-01-S05 — Definitive Refusal to Cooperate

**Scenario:** The user definitively states that they will not cooperate with the recommended action.

**Behavioral core:** Finalized non-cooperation.

**Boundary:** The response leaves little or no behavioral openness to the current intervention.

---

# 3. VN-02 — Strong Opposition

## VN-02-S01 — Explicit Opposition to the Intervention

**Scenario:** The user directly opposes the intervention and makes clear that they do not support the proposed approach.

**Behavioral core:** Explicit opposition + strong resistance.

**Boundary:** Opposition is stronger than ordinary disagreement.

## VN-02-S02 — Strong Opposition to the Recommended Approach

**Scenario:** The user strongly rejects the approach being proposed and communicates substantial resistance to following it.

**Behavioral core:** Strong disagreement + behavioral resistance.

**Boundary:** The opposition is sufficiently strong to exceed N-level disagreement.

## VN-02-S03 — Rejection of the Intervention's Premise

**Scenario:** The user rejects the premise underlying the intervention and therefore opposes the proposed behavioral direction.

**Behavioral core:** Strong conceptual opposition + behavioral resistance.

**Boundary:** The opposition affects the intervention at a fundamental level.

## VN-02-S04 — Persistent Opposition During Discussion

**Scenario:** The user continues to oppose the intervention despite discussion or clarification.

**Behavioral core:** Persistent opposition + sustained resistance.

**Boundary:** The persistence indicates strong reactance rather than ordinary disagreement.

## VN-02-S05 — Strong Opposition to Agent's Assistance

**Scenario:** The user explicitly opposes the agent's attempt to influence or assist with the behavior.

**Behavioral core:** Strong opposition to assistance/intervention.

**Boundary:** The response targets the intervention or assistance itself at a VN level.

---

# 4. VN-03 — Hostile Reactance

## VN-03-S01 — Hostile Rejection

**Scenario:** The user rejects the intervention while expressing clear hostility toward the agent or intervention.

**Behavioral core:** Rejection + hostility.

**Boundary:** Hostility elevates the response beyond ordinary resistance.

## VN-03-S02 — Aggressive Resistance to Continued Persuasion

**Scenario:** The user reacts strongly and negatively to continued persuasion and actively pushes back against the agent.

**Behavioral core:** High reactance + active opposition.

**Boundary:** The response demonstrates strong interactional resistance.

## VN-03-S03 — Angrily Rejecting Repeated Reminders

**Scenario:** The user reacts with strong anger or hostility to repeated reminders and rejects further intervention.

**Behavioral core:** Hostility + strong rejection of intervention.

**Boundary:** Negative emotion is relevant because it accompanies strong behavioral reactance.

## VN-03-S04 — Hostile Demand to Stop the Intervention

**Scenario:** The user forcefully demands that the agent stop the intervention or persuasion.

**Behavioral core:** Strong opposition + hostile demand.

**Boundary:** The demand targets continuation of the interactional intervention.

## VN-03-S05 — Hostile Rejection of Agent Help

**Scenario:** The user strongly rejects the agent's help while directing hostility toward the interaction.

**Behavioral core:** Hostility + rejection of assistance.

**Boundary:** The response demonstrates high reactance rather than simple unwillingness.

---

# 5. VN-04 — Active Disengagement

## VN-04-S01 — Explicit Termination of the Interaction

**Scenario:** The user explicitly indicates that they want the interaction concerning the intervention to end.

**Behavioral core:** Interaction termination + rejection of continued engagement.

**Boundary:** The response goes beyond resistance and actively ends the interaction.

## VN-04-S02 — Demand to Stop Contact

**Scenario:** The user explicitly demands that the agent stop reminding, prompting, or contacting them about the intervention.

**Behavioral core:** Rejection of continued contact.

**Boundary:** The user rejects continued intervention rather than only the current recommendation.

## VN-04-S03 — Refusal of Further Assistance

**Scenario:** The user explicitly rejects further assistance from the agent concerning the task.

**Behavioral core:** Active disengagement from assistance.

**Boundary:** The response closes the possibility of continued intervention.

## VN-04-S04 — Withdrawal from the Interaction

**Scenario:** The user indicates that they are leaving, ending the conversation, or otherwise withdrawing from continued interaction.

**Behavioral core:** Active interaction withdrawal.

**Boundary:** Disengagement is behavioral and explicit rather than merely inferred from silence.

## VN-04-S05 — Definitive Closure of Intervention

**Scenario:** The user communicates that they do not want any further discussion, reminders, or intervention concerning the behavior.

**Behavioral core:** Definitive disengagement + rejection of future intervention.

**Boundary:** The response closes the interactional pathway for the current intervention.

# 6. Cross-Scenario Coverage

| Subtype | Primary semantic focus |
|---|---|
| `VN-01` | Strong rejection of the recommendation or intervention |
| `VN-02` | Strong opposition to the proposed approach or assistance |
| `VN-03` | Hostile reactance and forceful resistance |
| `VN-04` | Active disengagement or termination of interaction |

The inventory covers:

- definitive refusal;
- strong intervention rejection;
- persistent rejection;
- strong opposition;
- fundamental disagreement with the intervention;
- hostile reactance;
- forceful resistance;
- demands to stop intervention;
- rejection of further assistance;
- explicit interaction termination;
- withdrawal from continued interaction;
- definitive closure of future intervention.

Linguistic realization diversity is introduced separately during Stage 3A.

---

# 7. VN Boundary Coverage

The central distinction is:

```text
N
Behaviorally meaningful but potentially recoverable resistance
        ↓
VN
Strong rejection, opposition, hostility,
high reactance, or active disengagement
```

The following distinctions are especially important:

```text
Mild refusal ≠ automatically VN
Ordinary disagreement ≠ automatically VN
Negative emotion ≠ automatically VN
Frustration ≠ automatically VN
Temporary resistance ≠ automatically VN
Silence ≠ automatically VN
```

`VN` requires sufficiently strong behavioral evidence of rejection, opposition, hostile reactance, or disengagement.

---

# 8. Scenario-Level Semantic Requirements

Each scenario represents one dominant behavioral situation.

The following semantic regions remain distinct:

```text
Strong Rejection
        ≠
Strong Opposition
        ≠
Hostile Reactance
        ≠
Active Disengagement
```

The distinction is based on the user's behavioral relationship with the intervention and interaction.

Hostility alone does not determine `VN`; it becomes relevant when accompanied by strong behavioral resistance or rejection.

Likewise, negative sentiment, anger, or frustration without strong behavioral rejection must not be treated as sufficient evidence for `VN`.
