# Stage 1 — Initial Semantic Anchors: VP

**Response Category:** `VP` — Very Positive  
**Semantic Subtypes:** 4  
**Semantic Scenarios:** 20  
**Initial Anchors:** 60

---

## 1. Purpose

This file defines initial natural-language anchor realizations for the `VP` Response Category.

`VP` represents a strongly positive behavioral reaction toward the intervention, characterized by strong acceptance, enthusiasm, appreciation, proactive engagement, or strong voluntary cooperation.

Each semantic scenario has three initial realizations:

1. **Direct** — the behavioral response is clearly expressed.
2. **Indirect** — the behavioral response is conveyed pragmatically without relying on an explicit category marker.
3. **Natural Conversational** — the response resembles ordinary spontaneous conversation.

The anchors are tied to their originating semantic scenarios so that scenario lineage is preserved.

# 7. Anchor Construction Principles

The anchors collectively represent:

- strong acceptance;
- enthusiasm;
- appreciation;
- proactive engagement;
- voluntary cooperation;
- strong commitment;
- positive reaction after explanation;
- positive reaction after personalization;
- interest in continuing;
- willingness to act without further prompting.

The three realizations for each scenario preserve the same underlying behavioral meaning while varying how explicitly that meaning is expressed.

The `VP` anchors are distinguished from `P` by the presence of stronger enthusiasm, appreciation, engagement, or voluntary cooperation. Ordinary acceptance without such evidence belongs to `P`.

The anchors do not rely on a single lexical marker to establish `VP`. Expressions such as “absolutely,” “thanks,” “definitely,” or “I’ll” are used in combination with behavioral context and should not independently determine the category.
