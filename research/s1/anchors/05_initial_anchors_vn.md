# Stage 1 — Initial Semantic Anchors: VN

**Response Category:** `VN` — Very Negative  
**Semantic Subtypes:** 4  
**Semantic Scenarios:** 20  
**Initial Anchors:** 60

---

## 1. Purpose

This file defines initial natural-language anchor realizations for the `VN` Response Category.

`VN` represents a strongly negative behavioral reaction toward the intervention characterized by strong rejection, explicit opposition, hostile reactance, or active disengagement.

Each semantic scenario has three initial realizations:

1. **Direct** — the behavioral response is clearly expressed.
2. **Indirect** — the behavioral response is conveyed pragmatically without relying on an explicit category marker.
3. **Natural Conversational** — the response resembles ordinary spontaneous conversation.

The anchors are tied to their originating semantic scenarios so that scenario lineage is preserved.

# 2. VN-01 — Strong Rejection

## VN-01-S01 — Explicit Rejection of the Recommendation

**Direct:**  
“No. I’m not doing that.”

**Indirect:**  
“That’s not something I’m going to agree to.”

**Natural Conversational:**  
“No, I’m not going to do that.”

---

## VN-01-S02 — Firm Rejection of the Intervention

**Direct:**  
“I reject that recommendation completely.”

**Indirect:**  
“That approach is not acceptable to me.”

**Natural Conversational:**  
“No, I’m not going with that approach.”

---

## VN-01-S03 — Repeated Rejection After Continued Intervention

**Direct:**  
“I’ve already said no, and I’m still not going to do it.”

**Indirect:**  
“Continuing to suggest it isn’t going to change my decision.”

**Natural Conversational:**  
“I already told you no. I’m not doing it.”

---

## VN-01-S04 — Rejection of Further Recommendation

**Direct:**  
“I do not want any more recommendations about this.”

**Indirect:**  
“Further suggestions on this are not welcome.”

**Natural Conversational:**  
“Please don’t keep giving me suggestions about this.”

---

## VN-01-S05 — Definitive Refusal to Cooperate

**Direct:**  
“I will not cooperate with this.”

**Indirect:**  
“There is no agreement from me on this.”

**Natural Conversational:**  
“I’m not going to cooperate with that.”

# 3. VN-02 — Strong Opposition

## VN-02-S01 — Explicit Opposition to the Intervention

**Direct:**  
“I strongly oppose this intervention.”

**Indirect:**  
“I’m completely against this approach.”

**Natural Conversational:**  
“No, I’m really against doing this.”

---

## VN-02-S02 — Strong Opposition to the Recommended Approach

**Direct:**  
“I strongly disagree with this approach and will not follow it.”

**Indirect:**  
“This approach goes against what I’m willing to do.”

**Natural Conversational:**  
“I really don’t agree with this approach, and I’m not following it.”

---

## VN-02-S03 — Rejection of the Intervention's Premise

**Direct:**  
“I completely reject the reasoning behind this recommendation.”

**Indirect:**  
“I don’t accept the premise that this is something I should do.”

**Natural Conversational:**  
“I don’t buy the reasoning behind this at all.”

---

## VN-02-S04 — Persistent Opposition During Discussion

**Direct:**  
“I still strongly oppose the recommendation, even after your explanation.”

**Indirect:**  
“Your explanation hasn’t changed my position on this.”

**Natural Conversational:**  
“I understand what you’re saying, but I’m still completely against it.”

---

## VN-02-S05 — Strong Opposition to Agent's Assistance

**Direct:**  
“I do not want your assistance with this.”

**Indirect:**  
“Your involvement is not something I want here.”

**Natural Conversational:**  
“I don’t want your help with this.”

# 4. VN-03 — Hostile Reactance

## VN-03-S01 — Hostile Rejection

**Direct:**  
“I’m telling you no. Stop pushing this.”

**Indirect:**  
“This continued pressure is completely unacceptable.”

**Natural Conversational:**  
“No. Stop pushing me about this.”

---

## VN-03-S02 — Aggressive Resistance to Continued Persuasion

**Direct:**  
“Stop trying to persuade me. I’m not doing it.”

**Indirect:**  
“More persuasion is only making me oppose this more strongly.”

**Natural Conversational:**  
“Stop trying to convince me. I said no.”

---

## VN-03-S03 — Angry Rejection of Repeated Reminders

**Direct:**  
“I’m angry that you keep reminding me. Stop.”

**Indirect:**  
“These repeated reminders are making me want nothing to do with this.”

**Natural Conversational:**  
“Seriously, stop reminding me. It’s getting really annoying.”

---

## VN-03-S04 — Hostile Demand to Stop the Intervention

**Direct:**  
“Stop this intervention immediately.”

**Indirect:**  
“I do not want this conversation to continue in this direction.”

**Natural Conversational:**  
“Enough. Stop bringing this up.”

---

## VN-03-S05 — Hostile Rejection of Agent Help

**Direct:**  
“I don’t want your help, and I want you to leave me alone.”

**Indirect:**  
“Your assistance is unwanted, and I do not want further involvement.”

**Natural Conversational:**  
“I don’t need your help. Just leave me alone.”

**Indirect:**

**Direct:**  
“Stop reminding me about this.”

**Indirect:**  
“I do not want any further contact about this issue.”

**Natural Conversational:**  
“Please stop contacting me about this.”

## VN-04-S03 — Refusal of Further Assistance

**Direct:**  
“I do not want any further assistance from you.”

**Indirect:**  
“I’ll handle this without any more help.”

**Natural Conversational:**  
“No thanks, I don’t want any more help with this.”

---

## VN-04-S04 — Withdrawal from the Interaction

**Direct:**  
“I’m leaving this conversation now.”

**Indirect:**  
“I’m not continuing this discussion.”

**Natural Conversational:**  
“I’m done here. I’m not talking about this anymore.”

**Natural Conversational:**  
“Just leave this alone. I don’t want to hear about it again.”

# 7. Anchor Construction Principles

The anchors collectively represent:

- strong rejection;
- definitive refusal;
- persistent opposition;
- strong disagreement;
- rejection of continued recommendations;
- rejection of assistance;
- hostile reactance;
- forceful resistance to persuasion;
- demands to stop intervention;
- explicit interaction termination;
- active withdrawal;
- definitive closure of future intervention.

The three realizations for each scenario preserve the same underlying behavioral meaning while varying how explicitly that meaning is expressed.

The `VN` anchors are distinguished from `N` by the strength and finality of the behavioral response. Mild refusal, ordinary disagreement, reluctance, and recoverable resistance do not by themselves establish `VN`.

Hostility is represented in `VN-03` only where it accompanies strong behavioral reactance or rejection. Negative emotion alone is insufficient.

Active disengagement is represented through explicit behavioral withdrawal or termination rather than inferred from short responses or silence.

The anchors should not be interpreted as lexical templates for `VN`. Words such as “no,” “stop,” “leave,” “never,” or strong negative expressions cannot independently determine the category without considering the behavioral meaning of the response.
