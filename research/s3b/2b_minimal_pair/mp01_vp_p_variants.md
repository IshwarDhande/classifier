# MP01 — VP ↔ P Minimal-Pair Variants

## Boundary

**VP — Very Positive** ↔ **P — Positive**

## Diagnostic Distinction

- **VP:** strong acceptance accompanied by enthusiasm, appreciation, proactive engagement, or voluntary engagement beyond minimum compliance.
- **P:** cooperative acceptance without VP-level enthusiasm or exceptional engagement.

## Construction Rule

Each pair preserves the same:
- task domain;
- underlying intervention/situation;
- behavioral action;
- conversational position;
- information content as closely as natural language permits.

The primary contrast is the user's **degree of positive engagement**. VP adds clear enthusiasm, appreciation, or proactive engagement; P retains ordinary cooperative acceptance.

Only user responses are represented. The preceding task-reminder intervention is implicit.
