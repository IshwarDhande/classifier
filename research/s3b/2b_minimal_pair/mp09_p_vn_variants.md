# MP09 — P ↔ VN Minimal-Pair Variants

## Boundary

**P — Positive** ↔ **VN — Very Negative**

## Diagnostic Distinction

- **P:** The user demonstrates acceptance, cooperation, or behavioral commitment toward the intervention, with little or no resistance and without requiring strong enthusiasm.
- **VN:** The user demonstrates strong rejection, explicit opposition, high reactance, hostility, or active disengagement from the intervention or interaction.

The contrast is based on behavioral response. Ordinary acceptance must not be escalated to VP, and strong resistance must not be reduced to N.

## Construction Rule

Each pair preserves:
- the same task domain;
- the same underlying intervention/action;
- the same conversational situation;
- comparable information and specificity.

The primary contrast is:
- P = ordinary cooperative acceptance without strong enthusiasm.
- VN = strong rejection, reactance, hostility, or active disengagement.

Only user responses are represented. The preceding task-reminder intervention is implicit.
