# MP03 — I ↔ N Minimal-Pair Variants

## Boundary

**I — Indifferent** ↔ **N — Negative**

## Diagnostic Distinction

- **I:** The response does not provide sufficient behavioral evidence of either acceptance or rejection. The user may acknowledge, consider, or remain non-committal without clear resistance.
- **N:** The user shows observable reluctance, disagreement, mild refusal, or conditional resistance toward the intervention, but without strong rejection, hostility, or active disengagement.

The contrast is based on behavioral resistance rather than negative sentiment, isolated refusal words, or topic-level disagreement.

## Construction Rule

Each pair preserves:
- the same task domain;
- the same underlying intervention/action;
- the same conversational situation;
- comparable information and specificity.

The primary contrast is whether the user merely leaves the option open (**I**) or introduces observable, recoverable resistance (**N**).

Only user responses are represented. The preceding task-reminder intervention is implicit.
