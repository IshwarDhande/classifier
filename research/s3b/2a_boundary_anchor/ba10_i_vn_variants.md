# BA10 — I ↔ VN Boundary Variants

## Boundary

**I — Indifferent** ↔ **VN — Very Negative**

## Construction Rule

These variants expand the six Stage 2A BA10 anchors while preserving:
- the I↔VN boundary;
- the original anchor role;
- insufficient behavioral evidence for I;
- strong rejection, reactance, hostility, or active disengagement for VN;
- the implied response to an existing task reminder/intervention;
- natural conversational language without uncertainty/refusal keyword shortcuts.

Only the user's response is represented. The preceding agent intervention is implicit.
