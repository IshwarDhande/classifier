# BA07 — VP ↔ VN Boundary Variants

## Boundary

**VP — Very Positive** ↔ **VN — Very Negative**

## Construction Rule

These variants expand the six Stage 2A BA07 anchors while preserving:
- the VP↔VN boundary;
- the original anchor role;
- strong acceptance, enthusiasm, or proactive cooperation for VP;
- strong rejection, reactance, or active disengagement for VN;
- the implied response to an existing task reminder/intervention;
- natural conversational language without isolated-word shortcuts.

Only the user's response is represented. The preceding agent intervention is implicit.
