# BA05 — VP ↔ I Boundary Variants

## Boundary

**VP — Very Positive** ↔ **I — Indifferent**

## Construction Rule

These variants expand the six Stage 2A BA05 anchors while preserving:
- the VP↔I boundary;
- the original anchor role;
- strong behavioral engagement/proactive cooperation for VP;
- insufficient behavioral commitment for I;
- the implied response to an existing task reminder/intervention;
- natural conversational language without lexical shortcuts.

Only the user's response is represented. The preceding agent intervention is implicit.
