## Boundary

`B03 — Not Started ↔ Completed`

**Diagnostic:** Is there evidence that the target task has been executed to completion?

Variants preserve the parent anchor's position while varying its linguistic realization.

## Boundary Invariant

- **Not Started:** no execution of the target task has begun.
- **Completed:** execution of the target task has finished.
- Preparation, readiness, and future intention do not establish execution.
- Completion must be grounded in evidence that the target task itself was carried out.
- Variants must not rely on scheduling, motivation, persuasion barriers, or dialogue purpose.
- `In Progress` is not represented by either side of this boundary; partially executed tasks belong to the adjacent B02 boundary.
