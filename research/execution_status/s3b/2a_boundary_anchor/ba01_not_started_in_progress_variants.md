# Stage 3B-A — Boundary Anchor Variants
## BA01: Not Started ↔ In Progress

### 1. Construction Scope

**Boundary:** `B01` — Not Started ↔ In Progress  
**Parent resource:** Stage 2A BA01 boundary anchors  
**Expansion target:** 6 variants per parent × 6 parents = **36 candidate variants**  
**Standard variation set:** `LEX`, `SYN`, `NAT`, `EXP`, `PRG`, `CMK`

The variants preserve the exact B01 execution distinction: **has execution of the target task actually begun?** Preparation, intention, and readiness remain Not Started; actual begun but unfinished execution remains In Progress. This follows the Stage 3B-A requirement to vary linguistic realization without moving semantic position. fileciteturn158file1L9-L17

### 3. Variant Resources

#### BA01-01

**BA01-01-V01 — `LEX` — A Prototype**
- **Execution Status:** `Not Started`
- **Target Task:** Medication
- **Closest Competing State:** `In Progress`
- **Context:** After a medication reminder, the user clearly reports that execution has not begun.
- **Utterance:** “I haven't begun taking the medication yet.”

**BA01-01-V02 — `SYN` — A Prototype**
- **Execution Status:** `Not Started`
- **Target Task:** Medication
- **Closest Competing State:** `In Progress`
- **Context:** After a medication reminder, the user reports non-initiation using a different syntactic structure.
- **Utterance:** “Taking the medication hasn't started for me yet.”

**BA01-01-V03 — `NAT` — A Prototype**
- **Execution Status:** `Not Started`
- **Target Task:** Medication
- **Closest Competing State:** `In Progress`
- **Context:** Natural conversational report that medication execution has not begun.
- **Utterance:** “Not yet—I've still not taken the medication.”

**BA01-01-V04 — `EXP` — A Prototype**
- **Execution Status:** `Not Started`
- **Target Task:** Medication
- **Closest Competing State:** `In Progress`
- **Context:** Execution state is expressed with direct evidence of non-initiation.
- **Utterance:** “The medication is still untouched; I haven't taken it.”

**BA01-01-V05 — `PRG` — A Prototype**
- **Execution Status:** `Not Started`
- **Target Task:** Medication
- **Closest Competing State:** `In Progress`
- **Context:** The user answers the reminder by reporting the current execution condition.
- **Utterance:** “As of now, I haven't started the medication.”

**BA01-01-V06 — `CMK` — A Prototype**
- **Execution Status:** `Not Started`
- **Target Task:** Medication
- **Closest Competing State:** `In Progress`
- **Context:** A conversational marker is added without changing the execution interpretation.
- **Utterance:** “Well, I haven't started taking the medication yet.”
