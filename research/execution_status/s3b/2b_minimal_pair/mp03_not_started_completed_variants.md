# Stage 3B-B — Minimal-Pair Variants: B03 Not Started ↔ Completed

## Boundary

**Boundary ID:** `B03`  
**State A:** `Not Started`  
**State B:** `Completed`

### Construction Principle

Each pair varies the linguistic realization while preserving:

- the same target task;
- the same broad practical situation;
- comparable information;
- the distinction between no begun execution and finished execution.

> **Change the execution evidence, not the task or situation.**

The six Stage-2 families are retained as lineage parents. Each parent receives six controlled variants.

---

## MP03-01 — Direct Non-Execution Versus Completion

**Parent:** `I haven't started taking the medication yet.` ↔ `I finished taking the medication.`  
**Target:** Medication

### Variant 01
- **Not Started:** “I haven't started taking the medication yet.”
- **Completed:** “I've finished taking the medication.”

### Variant 02
- **Not Started:** “I still haven't taken the medication.”
- **Completed:** “I already took the medication.”

### Variant 03

- **Completed:** “The medication has been taken in full.”

### Variant 04

- **Completed:** “I've completed the medication dose.”

### Variant 05
- **Not Started:** “No, I haven't taken it yet.”
- **Completed:** “Yes, I've taken it already.”

### Variant 06

## Pair-Level Validation Criteria

Every descendant pair must satisfy:

1. Both sides refer to the same target task.
2. The broad practical situation remains comparable.
3. `Not Started` contains no evidence that target-task execution has begun.
4. `Completed` contains evidence that target-task execution has finished.
5. Preparation is not treated as execution.
6. Future intention is not treated as execution.
7. Indirect object-state evidence must still clearly establish the execution state.
8. Non-execution or refusal must remain `Not Started` when no execution occurred.
9. The distinction must not depend on motivation, persuasion barriers, dialogue purpose, sentiment, or scheduling.
10. The examples must remain natural conversational language.
11. The contrast must not rely on a single mandatory keyword.
12. The pair must not introduce completion of a different task.
