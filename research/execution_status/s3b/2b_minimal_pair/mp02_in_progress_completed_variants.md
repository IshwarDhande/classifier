# Stage 3B-B — Minimal-Pair Variants: B02 In Progress ↔ Completed

## Boundary

**Boundary ID:** `B02`  
**State A:** `In Progress`  
**State B:** `Completed`

### Construction Principle

Each pair varies the linguistic realization while preserving:

- the same target task;
- the same broad practical situation;
- the same execution boundary;
- comparable information;
- the primary contrast between unfinished and finished execution.

> **Change the evidence of completion, not the task or situation.**

The six Stage-2 families are retained as lineage parents. Each parent receives six controlled variants.

---

## MP02-01 — Explicit Completion Evidence

**Parent:** `I'm still taking the medication; I haven't finished yet.` ↔ `I've finished taking the medication.`  
**Target:** Medication

### Variant 01
- **In Progress:** “I'm still taking the medication; I haven't finished the dose.”
- **Completed:** “I've finished taking the medication; the dose is complete.”

### Variant 02
- **In Progress:** “I'm taking the medication now, but I'm not done with it yet.”
- **Completed:** “I took the medication already; I'm done with it.”

### Variant 03
- **In Progress:** “The medication is still being taken—I haven't finished yet.”
- **Completed:** “The medication has been taken; I've finished the dose.”

### Variant 04
- **In Progress:** “I'm partway through taking the medication.”
- **Completed:** “I've completed taking the medication.”

### Variant 05
- **In Progress:** “I'm still on the medication dose; there's some left to take.”
- **Completed:** “There's nothing left to take—I finished the medication.”

### Variant 06
- **In Progress:** “Not done yet—I started the medication, but I'm still taking it.”
- **Completed:** “Done—I started and finished the medication.”

## Pair-Level Validation Criteria

Every descendant pair must satisfy:

1. Both sides refer to the same target task.
2. The broad practical situation remains comparable.
3. `In Progress` establishes that execution has begun.
4. `In Progress` establishes that execution remains unfinished.
5. `Completed` establishes that execution has finished.
6. Partial or near-complete execution remains `In Progress`.
7. No scheduling, motivation, persuasion barrier, dialogue purpose, or sentiment change determines the label.
8. The contrast is recoverable from execution evidence rather than one mandatory keyword.
9. The pair remains natural conversational language.
10. Variants do not introduce completion of a different task.
