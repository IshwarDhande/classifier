# Stage 3A — Linguistic Expansion: N-A

**Response Category:** `N` — Negative
**Phase:** 07 — N-A
**Scenario Range:** `N-01-S01` through `N-02-S05`
**Variants:** 60

## Production Definition

Each record is a Stage-3A linguistic realization of a frozen Stage-1 semantic scenario. The underlying behavioral appraisal reflects hesitation, reluctance, or mild refusal that remains behaviorally recoverable, distinct from Indifferent ambiguity and from Very Negative hostility or total rejection.

**Standard variant sequence:** `LEX`, `SYN`, `NAT`, `EXP`, `PRG`, `CMK`.

**Lineage:** `Response Category → Semantic Subtype → Semantic Scenario → Initial Anchor → Stage-3A Variant`.
