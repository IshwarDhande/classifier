# Stage 3A — Linguistic Expansion: N-B

**Response Category:** `N` — Negative
**Phase:** 08 — N-B
**Scenario Range:** `N-03-S01` through `N-04-S05`
**Variants:** 60

## Production Definition

Each record is a Stage-3A linguistic realization of a frozen Stage-1 semantic scenario. The underlying behavioral appraisal reflects disagreement, preference against recommendations, or conditional resistance that remains recoverable, without escalating to Very Negative hostility or unconditional rejection.

**Standard variant sequence:** `LEX`, `SYN`, `NAT`, `EXP`, `PRG`, `CMK`.

**Lineage:** `Response Category → Semantic Subtype → Semantic Scenario → Initial Anchor → Stage-3A Variant`.
