# Stage 3A — Linguistic Expansion: I-B

**Response Category:** `I` — Indifferent
**Phase:** 06 — I-B
**Scenario Range:** `I-03-S01` through `I-04-S05`
**Variants:** 60

## Production Definition

Each record is a Stage-3A linguistic realization of a frozen Stage-1 semantic scenario. The underlying behavioral appraisal reflects non-committal hesitation, deferral, or ambiguous communication where behavioral intention remains unresolved.

**Standard variant sequence:** `LEX`, `NAT`, `EXP`, `PRG`, `HES`, `QST`.

**Lineage:** `Response Category → Semantic Subtype → Semantic Scenario → Initial Anchor → Stage-3A Variant`.
