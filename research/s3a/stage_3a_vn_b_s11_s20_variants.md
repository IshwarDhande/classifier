# Stage 3A — Linguistic Expansion: VN-B

**Response Category:** `VN` — Very Negative
**Phase:** 10 — VN-B
**Scenario Range:** `VN-03-S01` through `VN-04-S05`
**Variants:** 60

## Production Definition

Each record is a Stage-3A linguistic realization of a frozen Stage-1 semantic scenario. The underlying behavioral appraisal reflects hostile reactance, aggressive pushback, explicit termination of the interaction, and definitive closure of the intervention.

**Standard variant sequence:** `LEX`, `SYN`, `NAT`, `EXP`, `PRG`, `DSC`.

**Lineage:** `Response Category → Semantic Subtype → Semantic Scenario → Initial Anchor → Stage-3A Variant`.
