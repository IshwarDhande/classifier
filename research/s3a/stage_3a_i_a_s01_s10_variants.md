# Stage 3A — Linguistic Expansion: I-A

**Response Category:** `I` — Indifferent
**Phase:** 05 — I-A
**Scenario Range:** `I-01-S01` through `I-02-S05`
**Variants:** 60

## Production Definition

Each record is a Stage-3A linguistic realization of a frozen Stage-1 semantic scenario. The underlying behavioral appraisal reflects neutral acknowledgement or indifference without clear acceptance or active refusal.

**Standard variant sequence:** `LEX`, `NAT`, `EXP`, `PRG`, `HES`, `QST`.

**Lineage:** `Response Category → Semantic Subtype → Semantic Scenario → Initial Anchor → Stage-3A Variant`.
