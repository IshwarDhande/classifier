# Stage 3A — Linguistic Expansion: P-B

**Response Category:** `P` — Positive
**Phase:** 04 — P-B
**Scenario Range:** `P-03-S01` through `P-04-S05`
**Variants:** 60

## Production Definition

Each record is a Stage-3A linguistic realization of a frozen Stage-1 semantic scenario. Acceptance, cooperation, or commitment remains behaviorally evident, while the realization does not introduce the unusually strong enthusiasm, appreciation, or proactive engagement required for `VP`.

**Standard variant sequence:** `LEX`, `SYN`, `NAT`, `EXP`, `LEN`, `CMK`.

**Lineage:** `Response Category → Semantic Subtype → Semantic Scenario → Initial Anchor → Stage-3A Variant`.
