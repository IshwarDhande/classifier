# MP09 — P ↔ VN Minimal Pairs

## Boundary

**P (Positive) ↔ VN (Very Negative)**

## Core distinction

- **P:** The user demonstrates acceptance, cooperation, or behavioral commitment toward the intervention, with little or no resistance and without requiring strong enthusiasm.
- **VN:** The user demonstrates strong rejection, explicit opposition, high reactance, hostility, or active disengagement from the intervention or interaction.

The contrast is based on behavioral response. Ordinary acceptance must not be escalated to VP, and strong resistance must not be reduced to N.

---

## MP09-01

**P:** “Okay, I’ll keep my medication beside my toothbrush so I remember it.”

**VN:** “No. I’m not putting my medication there, and I don’t want this suggestion again.”

---

## MP09-02

**P:** “Sure, I can take the walk after lunch.”

**VN:** “Absolutely not. Stop telling me to use my lunch break for a walk.”

## MP09-03

## MP09-04

## MP09-05

**P:** “Okay, I can keep a glass of water by my desk today.”

**VN:** “I don’t want you monitoring what I drink. Stop bringing up water and leave me alone.”

## MP09-06

**P:** “Alright, I’ll take my medication at the scheduled time this evening.”

**VN:** “Stop interfering with how I take my medicine. I don’t want any more instructions about it.”
