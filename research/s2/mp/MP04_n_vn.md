# MP04 — N ↔ VN Minimal Pairs

## Boundary

**N — Negative** ↔ **VN — Very Negative**

## Core distinction

- **N:** The user shows reluctance, disagreement, mild refusal, or conditional resistance toward the intervention, but the response remains potentially recoverable.
- **VN:** The user expresses strong rejection, explicit opposition, high reactance, hostility, or active disengagement from the intervention or interaction.

The contrast is based on the strength of behavioral resistance. A negative sentiment or simple refusal is not sufficient by itself for VN.

---

## MP04-01

**N:**  
“I’d rather keep my medication somewhere else.”

**VN:**  
“No. I’m not doing that, and I don’t want this suggestion brought up again.”

---

## MP04-02

**N:**  
“I’d prefer to keep my lunch break for myself rather than taking a walk.”

**VN:**  
“Absolutely not. Stop trying to get me to walk during my lunch break.”
