# MP02 — P ↔ I Minimal Pairs

## Boundary

**P — Positive** ↔ **I — Indifferent**

## Core distinction

- **P:** The user demonstrates genuine acceptance, cooperation, or behavioral commitment toward the intervention.
- **I:** The user acknowledges, considers, or responds to the intervention without sufficient evidence of acceptance, rejection, or behavioral commitment.

The contrast is based on behavioral evidence rather than sentiment, politeness, or isolated lexical cues.

---

## MP02-01

**P:**  
“I’ll keep my medication beside my toothbrush so I remember to take it.”

**I:**  
“That’s one place I could keep my medication.”

---

## MP02-02

**P:**  
“I can do that after lunch. I’ll take the walk today.”

**I:**  
“After lunch could be one possible time.”
