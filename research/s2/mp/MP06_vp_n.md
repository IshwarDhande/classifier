# MP06 — VP ↔ N Minimal Pairs

## Boundary

**VP (Very Positive) ↔ N (Negative)**

## Core distinction

- **VP:** The user demonstrates strong acceptance, enthusiasm, appreciation, or proactive cooperation toward the intervention.
- **N:** The user demonstrates reluctance, disagreement, mild refusal, or conditional resistance, without strong rejection, hostility, or active disengagement.

The contrast is based on behavioral orientation toward the intervention, not emotional tone or isolated lexical cues.

---

## MP06-01

**VP:** “That’s a great idea. I’ll put it there tonight and start using that setup right away.”

**N:** “I prefer keeping my medication in the cabinet rather than by the toothbrush.”

---

## MP06-02

**VP:** “Yes, that sounds excellent. I’ll take the walk after lunch tomorrow and get started.”

**N:** “Taking a walk during lunch doesn’t fit my plans today.”

---

## MP06-03

**VP:** “I really like that approach. I’ll put a bottle on my desk today and keep it there.”

**N:** “I don’t really want to keep a bottle on my desk while I work.”

---

## MP06-04

**VP:** “That could make things much easier. I’ll split them into two sessions today and see how it goes.”

**N:** “I’d prefer not to split the exercises; one session is easier for me to manage.”

---

## MP06-05

**VP:** “That’s a really useful idea. I’ll prepare everything tonight so I can start easily tomorrow.”

**N:** “Setting them out the night before doesn’t really work for me.”

---

## MP06-06

**VP:** “That really lowers the barrier for me. I’ll lay out my physiotherapy mat right now so I’m ready tonight.”

**N:** “I’d rather not leave my physiotherapy mat out on the floor before dinner.”
