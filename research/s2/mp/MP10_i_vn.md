# MP10 — I ↔ VN Minimal Pairs

## Boundary

**I (Indifferent) ↔ VN (Very Negative)**

## Core distinction

- **I:** The user acknowledges, considers, or responds to the intervention without sufficient behavioral evidence of either acceptance or rejection.
- **VN:** The user demonstrates strong rejection, explicit opposition, high reactance, hostility, or active disengagement from the intervention or interaction.

The contrast is based on behavioral evidence. A neutral acknowledgement must not be treated as rejection, and strong resistance must not be reduced to mere uncertainty.

---

## MP10-01

**I:** “Keeping it by the toothbrush is one option I could consider.”

**VN:** “I’m not putting my medication there, and I don’t want this suggestion brought up again.”
