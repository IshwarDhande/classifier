# MP07 — VP ↔ VN Minimal Pairs

## Boundary

**VP (Very Positive) ↔ VN (Very Negative)**

## Core distinction

- **VP:** The user demonstrates strong acceptance, enthusiasm, appreciation, and proactive cooperation toward the intervention.
- **VN:** The user demonstrates strong rejection, explicit opposition, high reactance, hostility, or active disengagement from the intervention or interaction.

The contrast is established through behavioral response and interactional stance, not merely positive or negative sentiment.

---

## MP07-01

**VP:** “That’s an excellent idea. I’ll set it up tonight, and I’m really glad we found something that can make this easier.”

**VN:** “No. I’m not doing that, and I don’t want you bringing up this suggestion again.”

---

## MP07-02

**VP:** “Yes, I really like that plan. I’ll start after lunch tomorrow, and I’m looking forward to trying it.”

**VN:** “No chance. Stop telling me how to spend my lunch break.”

---

## MP07-03

**VP:** “That’s a great idea. I’ll put one on my desk right now and start using it this afternoon.”

**VN:** “I’m not putting a bottle there. Stop telling me how I should manage this.”

---

## MP07-04

**VP:** “That sounds really helpful. I’ll try the two-session approach today and see how much better it feels.”

**VN:** “I refuse to split them into two sessions. Stop trying to micromanage my exercises.”

---

## MP07-05

**VP:** “Yes, absolutely. That’s an easy and effective change. I’ll pour a glass with my meal right now.”

**VN:** “Stop telling me when to drink water. I don’t want any more hydration advice from you.”

---

## MP07-06

**VP:** “That would be really useful. I’ll make sure I take the evening medication, and I’m happy to have a system that keeps me on track.”

**VN:** “I’m not changing how I handle my medication. Stop trying to manage it for me.”
