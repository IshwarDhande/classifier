# MP08 — P ↔ N Minimal Pairs

## Boundary

**P (Positive) ↔ N (Negative)**

## Core distinction

- **P:** The user demonstrates acceptance, cooperation, or behavioral commitment toward the intervention, with little or no resistance.
- **N:** The user demonstrates reluctance, disagreement, mild refusal, or conditional resistance toward the intervention, without strong rejection, hostility, or active disengagement.

The distinction is based on behavioral orientation toward the intervention rather than sentiment, politeness, or isolated lexical cues.

---

## MP08-01

**P:** “I’ll keep my medication beside my toothbrush so it’s easier to remember.”

**N:** “I’d rather keep my medication somewhere else, away from my toothbrush.”

---

## MP08-02

**P:** “Okay, I’ll take the walk after lunch.”

**N:** “I have other things scheduled during lunch, so I’d rather skip the walk.”

---

## MP08-03

**P:** “I’ll keep a bottle on my desk and use it during the afternoon.”

**N:** “I’d prefer to keep the bottle somewhere other than my desk.”

---

## MP08-04

**P:** “Alright, I’ll split the exercises into two sessions today.”

**N:** “Splitting them up doesn’t really suit my routine today.”

---

## MP08-05

**P:** “I’ll lay them out tonight so I can start easily tomorrow.”

**N:** “I prefer not to lay out my exercise clothes ahead of time.”

---

## MP08-06

**P:** “Alright, I can do the physiotherapy exercises before my evening routine.”

**N:** “That timing doesn’t really work for me; I’d rather do my physiotherapy exercises earlier.”
