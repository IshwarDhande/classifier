# BA01 — VP ↔ P Boundary Anchors

## Boundary

**VP — Very Positive** ↔ **P — Positive**

### Core distinction

- **VP:** The user shows strong acceptance, enthusiasm, appreciation, proactive engagement, or unusually strong voluntary cooperation.
- **P:** The user shows genuine acceptance and cooperation without strong enthusiasm or elevated engagement.

The distinction must be based on the user's **behavioral reaction to the agent's reminder/intervention**, not sentiment, politeness, isolated keywords, or punctuation.

> **Data protocol:** Each example below is the user's utterance only. The implicit preceding agent turn is a reminder to perform the relevant task.

---

## BA01-A01 — VP Prototype

**User:**  
“Absolutely. I’ll take my evening medication now so I can get it done and stay on track.”

**Label:** `VP`

---

## BA01-A02 — P Prototype

**User:**  
“Okay, I’ll take my evening medication tonight.”

**Label:** `P`

---

## BA01-A03 — VP-Leaning Boundary

**User:**  
“Yes, I’m going to fill my water bottle now and keep it beside me so I actually drink throughout the day.”

**Label:** `VP`

---

## BA01-A04 — P-Leaning Boundary

**User:**  
“Sure, I’ll keep the water bottle beside me and drink from it today.”

**Label:** `P`

---

## BA01-A05 — Implicit VP

**User:**  
“I’ll put my walking shoes by the door and head out as soon as dinner is over.”

**Label:** `VP`

---

## BA01-A06 — Implicit P

**User:**  
“Alright, I’ll take the walk after dinner.”

**Label:** `P`
