# BA06 — VP ↔ N Boundary Anchors

## Boundary

**VP — Very Positive** ↔ **N — Negative**

### Core distinction

- **VP:** Strong acceptance, enthusiasm, proactive engagement, or unusually strong voluntary cooperation.
- **N:** Behavioral resistance, reluctance, disagreement, or reduced willingness to follow the intervention, while remaining recoverable.

The distinction is based on the user's behavioral stance toward the intervention.

---

## BA06-A01 — VP Prototype

**User:** “Yes, that sounds great. I’ll do the first physiotherapy session now and the second after work.”

**Label:** `VP`

---

## BA06-A02 — N Prototype

**User:** “I’d rather not split my physiotherapy routine. I don’t want to change my schedule that way.”

**Label:** `N`

---

## BA06-A03 — VP-Leaning Boundary

**User:** “I like that idea. I’ll put my medication beside my breakfast items tonight so I start tomorrow.”

**Label:** `VP`

---

## BA06-A04 — N-Leaning Boundary

**User:** “I understand the suggestion, but I don’t want to keep my medication out on the breakfast counter.”

**Label:** `N`

---

## BA06-A05 — Implicit VP

**User:** “That would make drinking water much easier. I’ll fill up a large bottle and keep it beside my monitor.”

**Label:** `VP`

---

## BA06-A06 — Implicit N

**User:** “I know it could help with hydration, but I don’t want to crowd my desk with a water bottle.”

**Label:** `N`
