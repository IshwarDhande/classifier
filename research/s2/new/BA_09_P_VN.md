# BA09 — P ↔ VN Boundary Anchors

## Boundary

**P — Positive** ↔ **VN — Very Negative**

### Core distinction

- **P:** Ordinary acceptance or cooperation without strong enthusiasm.
- **VN:** Strong rejection, explicit opposition, high reactance, hostility, or active disengagement.

The distinction depends on behavioral reaction and interactional stance.

---

## BA09-A01 — P Prototype

**User:** “Okay, I can keep my medication beside my breakfast items.”

**Label:** `P`

---

## BA09-A02 — VN Prototype

**User:** “No. I’m not doing that, and I don’t want you telling me how to manage my medication again.”

**Label:** `VN`

---

## BA09-A03 — P-Leaning Boundary

**User:** “Alright, I’ll keep a water bottle there and see how that works for me.”

**Label:** `P`

---

## BA09-A04 — VN-Leaning Boundary

**User:** “I don’t want to hear suggestions about drinking water anymore. Stop bringing it up.”

**Label:** `VN`

---

## BA09-A05 — Implicit P

**User:** “That’s fine. I’ll go for a walk after dinner tonight.”

**Label:** `P`

---

## BA09-A06 — Implicit VN

**User:** “I’m done discussing walking. Don’t give me any more advice about exercise.”

**Label:** `VN`
