# BA02 — P ↔ I Boundary Anchors

## Boundary

**P — Positive** ↔ **I — Indifferent**

### Core distinction

- **P:** The user shows genuine behavioral acceptance or willingness to cooperate with the intervention.
- **I:** The user acknowledges, considers, or responds to the intervention without sufficient evidence of behavioral acceptance or rejection.

The boundary is determined by **behavioral commitment**, not by politeness, sentiment, or isolated lexical cues.

> **Data protocol:** Each example below is the user's utterance only. The implicit preceding agent turn is a reminder to perform the relevant task.

---

## BA02-A01 — P Prototype

**User:**  
“Okay, I’ll do my physiotherapy exercises before I settle down this evening.”

**Label:** `P`

---

## BA02-A02 — I Prototype

**User:**  
“I’ll think about whether I can fit the physiotherapy exercises into my evening.”

**Label:** `I`

---

## BA02-A03 — P-Leaning Boundary

**User:**  
“Yeah, that should work. I’ll take my medication after breakfast.”

**Label:** `P`

---

## BA02-A04 — I-Leaning Boundary

**User:**  
“Yeah, that might work. I’ll see how my breakfast routine goes.”

**Label:** `I`

---

## BA02-A05 — Implicit P

**User:**  
“Alright, I’ll keep the water bottle beside my desk and take regular sips today.”

**Label:** `P`

---

## BA02-A06 — Implicit I

**User:**  
“Keeping a water bottle nearby is something I can consider.”

**Label:** `I`
