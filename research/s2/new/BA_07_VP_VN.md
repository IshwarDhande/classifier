# BA07 — VP ↔ VN Boundary Anchors

## Boundary

**VP — Very Positive** ↔ **VN — Very Negative**

### Core distinction

- **VP:** Strong acceptance, enthusiasm, proactive engagement, or unusually strong voluntary cooperation.
- **VN:** Strong rejection, explicit opposition, high reactance, hostility, or active disengagement.

The distinction depends on interactional stance and degree of engagement.

---

## BA07-A01 — VP Prototype

**User:** “Yes, absolutely. I’ll take that walk after breakfast tomorrow, and I’m looking forward to getting started.”

**Label:** `VP`

---

## BA07-A02 — VN Prototype

**User:** “No. I don’t want to go for walks at all. Stop bringing this up with me.”

**Label:** `VN`

---

## BA07-A03 — VP-Leaning Boundary

**User:** “That’s a really useful idea. I’m going to unroll the physiotherapy mat tonight and try the exercises tomorrow.”

**Label:** `VP`

---

## BA07-A04 — VN-Leaning Boundary

**User:** “I don’t want suggestions about my physiotherapy anymore. Please stop telling me how I should do my exercises.”

**Label:** `VN`

---

## BA07-A05 — Implicit VP

**User:** “That solves the main problem for me. I’ll put the medication on my nightstand tonight and take it before bed.”

**Label:** `VP`

---

## BA07-A06 — Implicit VN

**User:** “I’ve had enough of your medication advice. Leave this alone and don’t bring it up again.”

**Label:** `VN`
