# BA08 — P ↔ N Boundary Anchors

## Boundary

**P — Positive** ↔ **N — Negative**

### Core distinction

- **P:** Ordinary acceptance or cooperation without strong enthusiasm or exceptional engagement.
- **N:** Reluctance, disagreement, refusal, or reduced willingness to follow the intervention, while remaining recoverable.

The distinction is based on behavioral acceptance versus behavioral resistance.

---

## BA08-A01 — P Prototype

**User:** “Sure. I can keep a water bottle there and take sips during the morning.”

**Label:** `P`

---

## BA08-A02 — N Prototype

**User:** “I’d rather not keep a water bottle on my desk. I prefer getting a cup when I need one.”

**Label:** `N`

---

## BA08-A03 — P-Leaning Boundary

**User:** “Alright, I can take a walk after lunch today.”

**Label:** `P`

---

## BA08-A04 — N-Leaning Boundary

**User:** “I could, but I don’t really feel like taking a walk today.”

**Label:** `N`

---

## BA08-A05 — Implicit P

**User:** “That works for me. I’ll do the physiotherapy before I settle down for the evening.”

**Label:** `P`

---

## BA08-A06 — Implicit N

**User:** “That timing doesn’t work for me. I’d rather leave the physiotherapy for another part of the day.”

**Label:** `N`
