# BA05 — VP ↔ I Boundary Anchors

## Boundary

**VP — Very Positive** ↔ **I — Indifferent**

### Core distinction

- **VP:** The user shows strong acceptance, enthusiasm, appreciation, proactive engagement, or unusually strong voluntary cooperation.
- **I:** The user acknowledges, considers, or responds to the intervention without sufficient evidence of behavioral acceptance or rejection.

The distinction depends on **behavioral commitment and engagement**, not on sentiment, politeness, or isolated lexical cues.

> **Data protocol:** Each example below is the user's utterance only. The implicit preceding agent turn is a reminder to perform the relevant task.

---

## BA05-A01 — VP Prototype

**User:**  
“That’s exactly what I needed. I’m putting my medication beside my breakfast bowl right now.”

**Label:** `VP`

---

## BA05-A02 — I Prototype

**User:**  
“That’s one option. I’ll think about whether keeping my medication there works for me.”

**Label:** `I`

---

## BA05-A03 — VP-Leaning Boundary

**User:**  
“That could really help. I’m going to keep a fresh water bottle beside me starting today.”

**Label:** `VP`

---

## BA05-A04 — I-Leaning Boundary

**User:**  
“That could help. I’ll keep the water-bottle idea in mind and see whether I try it.”

**Label:** `I`

---

## BA05-A05 — Implicit VP

**User:**  
“I like that approach. I’ll have my walking shoes ready so I can head out right after lunch.”

**Label:** `VP`

---

## BA05-A06 — Implicit I

**User:**  
“That’s worth considering. I haven’t decided what I’ll do during lunch yet.”

**Label:** `I`
