# BA10 — I ↔ VN Boundary Anchors

## Boundary

**I — Indifferent** ↔ **VN — Very Negative**

### Core distinction

- **I:** Insufficient behavioral evidence of acceptance or rejection; the user may acknowledge, defer, remain uncertain, or leave the decision open.
- **VN:** Strong rejection, explicit opposition, high reactance, hostility, or active disengagement.

The distinction depends on behavioral evidence and interactional stance.

---

## BA10-A01 — I Prototype

**User:** “I haven’t decided yet. I’ll see how my afternoon goes before deciding on the physiotherapy exercises.”

**Label:** `I`

---

## BA10-A02 — VN Prototype

**User:** “I’m not doing that. Stop telling me to do physiotherapy.”

**Label:** `VN`

---

## BA10-A03 — I-Leaning Boundary

**User:** “I’m not sure. I’ll think about whether I can fit the evening medication into my routine.”

**Label:** `I`

---

## BA10-A04 — VN-Leaning Boundary

**User:** “I don’t want any more reminders about my medication. Please stop bringing it up.”

**Label:** `VN`

---

## BA10-A05 — Implicit I

**User:** “That’s something I can consider. I’ll decide later what hydration setup works for me.”

**Label:** `I`

---

## BA10-A06 — Implicit VN

**User:** “I’ve heard enough suggestions about drinking water. Leave it alone and don’t bring it up again.”

**Label:** `VN`
