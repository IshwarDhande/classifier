# BA04 — N ↔ VN Boundary Anchors

## Boundary

**N — Negative** ↔ **VN — Very Negative**

### Core distinction

- **N:** The user expresses reluctance, disagreement, or conditional resistance toward the intervention, while remaining open to future cooperation.
- **VN:** The user demonstrates strong rejection, explicit opposition, high psychological reactance, hostility, or active disengagement from the intervention or assistant.

The distinction depends on **interactional stance and degree of resistance**, not on negative emotion or the presence of refusal-related words.

> **Data protocol:** Each example below is the user's utterance only. The implicit preceding agent turn is a reminder to perform the relevant task.

---

## BA04-A01 — N Prototype

**User:**  
“I’d rather not keep a water bottle beside me today. Maybe I’ll try that another time.”

**Label:** `N`

---

## BA04-A02 — VN Prototype

**User:**  
“No. I don’t want reminders about drinking, and I don’t want this brought up again.”

**Label:** `VN`

---

## BA04-A03 — N-Leaning Boundary

**User:**  
“I’d prefer not to go for a walk outside today. I can do some light activity indoors instead.”

**Label:** `N`

---

## BA04-A04 — VN-Leaning Boundary

**User:**  
“Stop telling me to go for a walk. I don’t want these suggestions anymore.”

**Label:** `VN`

---

## BA04-A05 — Implicit N

**User:**  
“I don’t think doing the physiotherapy exercises before breakfast will work for me. I’d rather do them later.”

**Label:** `N`

---

## BA04-A06 — Implicit VN

**User:**  
“I’ve already said no. Leave my physiotherapy exercises alone and don’t bring them up again.”

**Label:** `VN`
