# BA03 — I ↔ N Boundary Anchors

## Boundary

**I — Indifferent** ↔ **N — Negative**

### Core distinction

- **I:** The user does not provide sufficient evidence of acceptance or rejection. The response may acknowledge, defer, remain uncertain, or leave the decision open.
- **N:** The user expresses behavioral resistance, reluctance, disagreement, or reduced willingness to follow the intervention.

The distinction depends on **behavioral resistance**, not merely uncertainty, hesitation, negative emotion, or the presence of refusal-related words.

---

## BA03-A01 — I Prototype

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Could you take a short walk after lunch today?”

**User:**  
“I’m not sure yet. I’ll decide later how my afternoon looks.”

**Label:** `I`

---

## BA03-A02 — N Prototype

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Could you take a short walk after lunch today?”

**User:**  
“Not today. I don’t want to take a walk during lunch.”

**Label:** `N`

---

## BA03-A03 — I-Leaning Boundary

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Would you like to do your physiotherapy exercises before dinner this evening?”

**User:**  
“I’m still deciding whether I’ll have enough energy for them before dinner.”

**Label:** `I`

---

## BA03-A04 — N-Leaning Boundary

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Would you like to do your physiotherapy exercises before dinner this evening?”

**User:**  
“I have the time, but I’d rather not do the physiotherapy exercises before dinner.”

**Label:** `N`

---

## BA03-A05 — Implicit I

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Would you be willing to keep your medication beside your morning tea so it is easier to remember?”

**User:**  
“Let’s leave that open. I’ll see how my tea setup looks in the morning.”

**Label:** `I`

---

## BA03-A06 — Implicit N

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Would you be willing to keep your medication beside your morning tea so it is easier to remember?”

**User:**  
“Let’s skip that for now. I can manage my medication without putting it by my tea.”

**Label:** `N`
