# BA08 — P ↔ N Boundary Anchors

## Boundary

**P — Positive** ↔ **N — Negative**

### Core distinction

- **P:** The user accepts or cooperates with the intervention without strong enthusiasm or exceptional engagement.
- **N:** The user shows reluctance, disagreement, refusal, or reduced willingness to follow the intervention, while the interaction remains recoverable.

The distinction is based on **behavioral acceptance versus behavioral resistance**, not on whether the wording sounds polite, positive, or negative.

---

## BA08-A01 — P Prototype

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Would you be willing to keep a water bottle on your desk to sip from during the morning?”

**User:**  
“Sure. I can keep a water bottle there and take sips in the morning.”

**Label:** `P`

---

## BA08-A02 — N Prototype

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Would you be willing to keep a water bottle on your desk to sip from during the morning?”

**User:**  
“I’d rather not keep a water bottle on my desk. I prefer getting a cup when I need one.”

**Label:** `N`

---

## BA08-A03 — P-Leaning Boundary

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Could you take a short walk after lunch today?”

**User:**  
“Alright, I can take a walk after lunch today.”

**Label:** `P`

---

## BA08-A04 — N-Leaning Boundary

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Could you take a short walk after lunch today?”

**User:**  
“I could, but I don’t really feel like taking a walk today.”

**Label:** `N`

---

## BA08-A05 — Implicit P

**Context:**  
Assistant: “You can make your physiotherapy easier to fit in by doing it before your evening routine.”

**User:**  
“That works for me. I’ll do the physiotherapy before I settle down for the evening.”

**Label:** `P`

---

## BA08-A06 — Implicit N

**Context:**  
Assistant: “You can make your physiotherapy easier to fit in by doing it before your evening routine.”

**User:**  
“That timing doesn’t work for me. I’d rather leave the physiotherapy for another part of the day.”

**Label:** `N`
