# BA02 — P ↔ I Boundary Anchors

## Boundary

**P — Positive** ↔ **I — Indifferent**

### Core distinction

- **P:** The user shows genuine behavioral acceptance or willingness to cooperate with the intervention.
- **I:** The user acknowledges, considers, or responds to the intervention without sufficient evidence of behavioral acceptance or rejection.

The boundary is determined by **behavioral commitment**, not by politeness, sentiment, or isolated lexical cues.

---

## BA02-A01 — P Prototype

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Try doing your physiotherapy exercises before you settle down for the evening.”

**User:**  
“Okay, I’ll do the physiotherapy exercises before settling down.”

**Label:** `P`

---

## BA02-A02 — I Prototype

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Try doing your physiotherapy exercises before you settle down for the evening.”

**User:**  
“I’ll think about whether doing them then fits my evening.”

**Label:** `I`

---

## BA02-A03 — P-Leaning Boundary

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Would you be willing to take your medication after breakfast each morning?”

**User:**  
“Yeah, that should be manageable. I’ll take it after breakfast.”

**Label:** `P`

---

## BA02-A04 — I-Leaning Boundary

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Would you be willing to take your medication after breakfast each morning?”

**User:**  
“Yeah, that might be manageable. I’ll see how my breakfast routine goes.”

**Label:** `I`

---

## BA02-A05 — Implicit P

**Context:**  
Assistant: “You could keep a water bottle beside your desk and take small sips regularly to meet your hydration target.”

**User:**  
“Alright. I’ll set the water bottle up by my desk so I stay on track.”

**Label:** `P`

---

## BA02-A06 — Implicit I

**Context:**  
Assistant: “You could keep a water bottle beside your desk and take small sips regularly to meet your hydration target.”

**User:**  
“Alright. Keeping a water bottle nearby is something I can consider.”

**Label:** `I`
