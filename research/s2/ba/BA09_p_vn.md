# BA09 — P ↔ VN Boundary Anchors

## Boundary

**P — Positive** ↔ **VN — Very Negative**

### Core distinction

- **P:** The user shows ordinary acceptance or cooperation with the intervention, without requiring strong enthusiasm.
- **VN:** The user shows strong rejection, explicit opposition, high reactance, hostility, or active disengagement.

The distinction depends on the user's **behavioral reaction and interactional stance**, not on isolated positive or negative words.

---

## BA09-A01 — P Prototype

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Would you like to keep your medication beside your breakfast items so it is easy to remember?”

**User:**  
“Okay, I can keep my medication beside my breakfast items.”

**Label:** `P`

---

## BA09-A02 — VN Prototype

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Would you like to keep your medication beside your breakfast items so it is easy to remember?”

**User:**  
“No. I’m not doing that, and I don’t want you managing my medication again.”

**Label:** `VN`

---

## BA09-A03 — P-Leaning Boundary

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Would you like to keep a filled water bottle on your desk during work hours?”

**User:**  
“Alright, I’ll keep a water bottle there and see how that works for me.”

**Label:** `P`

---

## BA09-A04 — VN-Leaning Boundary

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Would you like to keep a filled water bottle on your desk during work hours?”

**User:**  
“I don’t want to hear suggestions about drinking water anymore. Stop bringing it up.”

**Label:** `VN`

---

## BA09-A05 — Implicit P

**Context:**  
Assistant: “You could take a short 10-minute walk outside after dinner to unwind.”

**User:**  
“That’s fine. I’ll go for a walk after dinner tonight.”

**Label:** `P`

---

## BA09-A06 — Implicit VN

**Context:**  
Assistant: “You could take a short 10-minute walk outside after dinner to unwind.”

**User:**  
“I’m done discussing walking. Don’t give me any more advice about exercise.”

**Label:** `VN`
