# BA07 — VP ↔ VN Boundary Anchors

## Boundary

**VP — Very Positive** ↔ **VN — Very Negative**

### Core distinction

- **VP:** The user shows strong acceptance, enthusiasm, appreciation, proactive engagement, or unusually strong voluntary cooperation.
- **VN:** The user demonstrates strong rejection, explicit opposition, high psychological reactance, hostility, or active disengagement from the intervention or assistant.

The distinction depends on the user's **interactional stance and degree of engagement**, not on isolated positive or negative words.

---

## BA07-A01 — VP Prototype

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Would you like to try a short walk around the neighbourhood after breakfast tomorrow?”

**User:**  
“Yes, absolutely. That would make getting active much easier. I’ll set an alarm for it right now.”

**Label:** `VP`

---

## BA07-A02 — VN Prototype

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Would you like to try a short walk around the neighbourhood after breakfast tomorrow?”

**User:**  
“No. I don’t want to go for walks at all. Stop bringing this up with me.”

**Label:** `VN`

---

## BA07-A03 — VP-Leaning Boundary

**Context:**  
Assistant: “You could keep your physiotherapy mat unrolled by the door so starting your exercises takes less effort.”

**User:**  
“That’s a really useful idea. I’m going to unroll the physiotherapy mat tonight and try it tomorrow.”

**Label:** `VP`

---

## BA07-A04 — VN-Leaning Boundary

**Context:**  
Assistant: “You could keep your physiotherapy mat unrolled by the door so starting your exercises takes less effort.”

**User:**  
“I don’t want suggestions about my physiotherapy anymore. Please stop telling me how I should do my exercises.”

**Label:** `VN`

---

## BA07-A05 — Implicit VP

**Context:**  
Assistant: “If your schedule is busy, you could place your evening medication right beside your bedside lamp.”

**User:**  
“That solves the main problem for me. I’ll put the medication on my nightstand tonight and take it first thing before bed.”

**Label:** `VP`

---

## BA07-A06 — Implicit VN

**Context:**  
Assistant: “If your schedule is busy, you could place your evening medication right beside your bedside lamp.”

**User:**  
“I’ve had enough of your medication advice. Leave this alone and don’t bring it up again.”

**Label:** `VN`
