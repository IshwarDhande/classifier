# BA10 — I ↔ VN Boundary Anchors

## Boundary

**I — Indifferent** ↔ **VN — Very Negative**

### Core distinction

- **I:** The user provides insufficient behavioral evidence of either acceptance or rejection. The user may acknowledge, defer, remain uncertain, or leave the decision open.
- **VN:** The user shows strong rejection, explicit opposition, high reactance, hostility, or active disengagement from the intervention or interaction.

The distinction depends on **behavioral evidence and interactional stance**, not on uncertainty, negative emotion, or isolated refusal-related wording.

---

## BA10-A01 — I Prototype

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Would you like to try splitting your physiotherapy exercises into two shorter sessions today?”

**User:**  
“I haven’t decided yet. I’ll see how my afternoon goes before deciding on the exercises.”

**Label:** `I`

---

## BA10-A02 — VN Prototype

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Would you like to try splitting your physiotherapy exercises into two shorter sessions today?”

**User:**  
“I’m not doing that. Stop asking me to do physiotherapy.”

**Label:** `VN`

---

## BA10-A03 — I-Leaning Boundary

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Would it help if I reminded you about your evening medication this evening?”

**User:**  
“I’m not sure. Let me think about whether I want a medication reminder.”

**Label:** `I`

---

## BA10-A04 — VN-Leaning Boundary

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Would it help if I reminded you about your evening medication this evening?”

**User:**  
“I don’t want any more reminders. Please stop offering medication alerts.”

**Label:** `VN`

---

## BA10-A05 — Implicit I

**Context:**  
Assistant: “You could keep a water bottle on your desk so it is easy to stay hydrated during work.”

**User:**  
“That’s something I can consider. I’ll decide later what hydration setup works for me.”

**Label:** `I`

---

## BA10-A06 — Implicit VN

**Context:**  
Assistant: “You could keep a water bottle on your desk so it is easy to stay hydrated during work.”

**User:**  
“I’ve heard enough suggestions about drinking water. Leave it alone and don’t bring it up again.”

**Label:** `VN`
