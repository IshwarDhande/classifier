# BA04 — N ↔ VN Boundary Anchors

## Boundary

**N — Negative** ↔ **VN — Very Negative**

### Core distinction

- **N:** The user expresses reluctance, disagreement, or conditional resistance toward the intervention, while remaining open to future cooperation.
- **VN:** The user demonstrates strong rejection, explicit opposition, high psychological reactance, hostility, or active disengagement from the intervention or assistant.

The distinction depends on **interactional stance and degree of resistance**, not on negative emotion or the presence of refusal-related words.

---

## BA04-A01 — N Prototype

**Context:**  
Assistant: “You could keep a fresh water bottle by your side to help meet your hydration target today.”

**User:**  
“I’d rather not have a water bottle on my desk today. Maybe later.”

**Label:** `N`

---

## BA04-A02 — VN Prototype

**Context:**  
Assistant: “You could keep a fresh water bottle by your side to help meet your hydration target today.”

**User:**  
“No. I don’t want you tracking my drinking, and I don’t want to discuss hydration again.”

**Label:** `VN`

---

## BA04-A03 — N-Leaning Boundary

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Would you be open to a short walk outside this afternoon to get moving?”

**User:**  
“I’d prefer not to go for a walk outside today. I can do some light activity inside instead.”

**Label:** `N`

---

## BA04-A04 — VN-Leaning Boundary

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Would you be open to a short walk outside this afternoon to get moving?”

**User:**  
“Stop telling me to go outside. I don’t want your walk suggestions anymore.”

**Label:** `VN`

---

## BA04-A05 — Implicit N

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Could we try doing the physiotherapy exercises before breakfast tomorrow?”

**User:**  
“I don’t think that timing is going to work for me. I’d rather keep the exercises later in the day.”

**Label:** `N`

---

## BA04-A06 — Implicit VN

**Context:**  
Assistant: “Could we try doing the physiotherapy exercises before breakfast tomorrow?”

**User:**  
“I’ve already said no. Please leave my exercises alone and don’t bring them up again.”

**Label:** `VN`
