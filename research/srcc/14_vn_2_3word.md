- Exactly 20 expressions.
- Every expression contains 2–3 words.
- Every expression is standalone.
- No conversational context is required.
- No task-specific wording is used.
- Each expression has one fixed SRCC category: VN.
- No expression is assigned to another SRCC category.
- The expressions are intended to represent strong rejection, strong opposition, or active disengagement rather than ordinary resistance.
